The idea

A short list, with the reasoning attached.

The name is the standard. Best of West Coast means a curated set of choices for people planning a vacation, a road trip, a weekend, or a closer look at a place nearby.

Three states.

California, Oregon, and Washington are the whole map. Lake Tahoe is included on the California side. The Columbia River Gorge is included where it lies in Oregon or Washington.

Comparisons with a point of view.

A useful guide says which option fits which traveler, and what you give up. That is the job of the lodging pages, the itineraries, and the decision guides.

Checked, then published.

Hours, prices, safety, access, and transportation need a source and a date. Until then the page stays labeled Guide coming soon.

Editorial standards

How a guide earns its place.

The full rules are on the about page. These four are the ones that shape every hub you can open today.

01

The map stays three states wide.

A place outside California, Oregon, and Washington is out of scope, including the Nevada side of Lake Tahoe.

02

A pick explains itself.

A published recommendation says who it suits and what the trade-off is.

03

An editor approves the page.

Research and drafts may be AI-assisted. A person approves the facts, the distinctions, and the images.

04

Unfinished work stays labeled.

If the reporting is not ready, the page says Guide coming soon and stops there.

A weekend in Portland

Two nights if you want the center and one neighborhood. Three if you want a real park morning.

Two nights if you want the center and one neighborhood. Three if you want a park morning that is not the rose garden. Sleep on the inner east side or in the Pearl. Read where to stay before you book the Old Town hotel by accident. The stops are in things to do.

Day 1: one park, then the bookstore

  • MAX to Washington Park. Roses first. They are free. Add the Japanese Garden only if you bought a ticket. Do not add the zoo unless the children are the trip.
  • Back downtown for Powell’s, and the art museum if it is not Monday.
  • Dinner is not downtown. Screen Door on Burnside if you can get in, or Kann if you already have the reservation. If you are on Hawthorne, Apizza Scholls from 5 p.m., and go early on Friday or Saturday because the shop says the dough sells out.

Day 2: a street, not another museum

Hawthorne and Division if you slept nearby, or Alberta if you did not. Mississippi if the Reddit thread that sent you there was about bars and tattoos rather than roses. Saturday only: add the Saturday Market at 2 SW Naito for an hour, then leave Old Town. Lan Su is the longer Old Town stop, if the garden’s site shows it open.

Skip a second pass through Pioneer Courthouse Square. You already know what downtown looks like.

Day 3: woods, or the airport

Forest Park from Lower Macleay if the legs are fresh and the closure page is clear. Pittock if you want the house and will check that day’s hours. If the flight is the same day, do not start a hike. Breakfast on the east side, Red Line from downtown, and check the last train the night before.

If you only have two days

Day 1 and day 2. Drop Forest Park. Drop the zoo. Keep one dinner you booked.

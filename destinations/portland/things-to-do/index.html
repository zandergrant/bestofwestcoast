Oregon · Portland

Best things to do in Portland

A downtown day is not the trip. Roses in the morning, dinner on the east side.

The mistake is treating downtown as the trip. On r/askportland, the recurring advice for a week of food, bars, and walking is inner Southeast or a north street such as Mississippi, not the hotel core. A January 2026 thread about nightlife landed on Buckman, Kerns, and Mississippi; several people called NW 23rd pretty and then quiet after dark. A June 2024 thread is sharper: some commenters tell visitors to skip Old Town and a dead-feeling downtown entirely. Others still say the Pearl is the easy hotel base. Both can be true. Sleep where you want your evenings. Use downtown for one day of the museum, the river, and Powell’s.

If you only have one day

Morning in Washington Park, afternoon downtown, dinner on the east side. Do not add Forest Park. Take MAX Red or Blue to Washington Park. Parking there is paid and, the city says, very limited on sunny weekends.

  • International Rose Test Garden, 400 SW Kingston. Free, per the parks brochure. Bloom is late May through October. This is the Washington Park stop that does not need a ticket.
  • Skip the Japanese Garden and the zoo on a one-day trip unless one of them is the reason you came. They are separate tickets, next to the roses, and they eat the afternoon. The garden is at 611 SW Kingston and posts its own hours, including an earlier window for members. The zoo, 4001 SW Canyon Road, uses timed entry and wants every person, including infants, on the reservation.
  • Powell’s City of Books, 1005 W Burnside, a city block on the edge of the Pearl. Powell’s lists regular hours of 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. It is worth the stop. It is not worth skipping dinner for.
  • If the museum is open, the Portland Art Museum at 1219 SW Park lists 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Sunday and Tuesday through Saturday, closed Monday. Rainy day: do the museum and Powell’s and drop the park.

What people get wrong

They build the day around Old Town. Lan Su Chinese Garden is the reason to go there, not the blocks around it. Yelp’s listing places it at 239 NW Everett Street. Hours on a review site are not the garden’s hours. The garden’s own site did not load for this check, so confirm the ticket and the time before you promise either.

Avg 4.6 Google 4.7 (3,810) · Yelp 4.4 (740) · Tripadvisor 4.6 (2,606) checked Oct 2026

Portland Saturday Market is the other Old Town errand, and only on Saturday. The market’s own page says it is open 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Saturdays through December 19, 2026, at 2 SW Naito Parkway, and that everything sold is handmade. The FAQ says Saturdays from March through Christmas Eve, not Sundays. Go if you are already downtown on a Saturday. Do not fly in for it.

Avg 4.3 Google 4.5 (4,935) · Yelp 4.0 (642) · Tripadvisor 4.3 (1,123) checked Oct 2026

The other miss is stacking every Washington Park ticket. Roses are the free view. The Japanese Garden is the paid, quieter garden. The zoo is a family day. Pick one paid thing.

Eat where the neighborhood is the point

Screen Door, east Burnside

Southern brunch and dinner at 2337 E Burnside, the address on its Yelp and Tripadvisor listings. This is the meal people wait for. Tripadvisor reviews from 2026 talk about a lunch line, huge portions, and street parking on the side streets rather than the pay lots. A Yelp question thread is people failing to find a Sunday brunch reservation. Go early, or book. Do not assume you will walk in at noon on a weekend.

Avg 4.7 Google 4.7 (7,834) · Yelp 4.5 (9,091) · Tripadvisor 4.6 (1,463) · OpenTable 4.9 (1,399) checked Oct 2026

Skip a downtown hotel breakfast for this, if brunch is the meal you care about. Trade-off: the wait, and a menu that is heavy on purpose.

Apizza Scholls, Hawthorne

4741 SE Hawthorne, and the shop’s own site says dine-in and takeout daily from 5 p.m., last seating 8:45, phones from 4 p.m., and that Friday and Saturday dough often sells out. Tables are first come. Tripadvisor regulars say the same thing in fewer words: go early, there is a line, the pepperoni is why. A minority of reviews call it overhyped. The line is the local tell, not a guarantee you will love the crust.

Avg 4.4 Google 4.6 (2,288) · Yelp 4.2 (1,947) · Tripadvisor 4.3 (341) checked Oct 2026

Do this instead of a random downtown pie if you are staying on Hawthorne or Division. Trade-off: no lunch, and a weekend night can end in sold out.

Kann, when it is a special night

Haitian cooking at 548 SE Ash, the address on the Yelp listing. Axios Portland, in June 2026, still had it living up to the hype and named the griyo, and also said the room is loud, the bill is a special-occasion bill, and a 4 p.m. table is the realistic way in. A Tripadvisor review from October 2026 praised the food and asked them to do something about the noise. Yelp’s score is high. Tripadvisor’s page, when checked, showed 31 reviews and no clear overall bubble score, so that score is left off.

Avg 4.5 Google 4.5 (1,297) · Yelp 4.4 (612) checked Oct 2026

Skip if you want a conversation and a moderate check. Do this if you want one meal you will talk about and you booked it.

Le Pigeon, if you want the tasting menu

738 E Burnside, chef’s tasting menu, reservations pushed on the restaurant’s Tripadvisor listing. The scores agree that the food is the draw. The complaints that repeat are the bill and the noise from upstairs. One March 2026 Tripadvisor review describes music loud enough that a couple could not hear each other. Believe that before you book an anniversary if quiet is the point.

The park and the hills, without pretending they are downtown

Forest Park is 5,200 acres and more than 80 miles of trail. It is a half day, not a stop on the way to Powell’s. Lines 15, 26, and 77 reach Lower Macleay. Check closures. Pittock Mansion, 3229 NW Pittock Drive, charges admission and posts seasonal hours. The September schedule on the site was not treated as October’s. OMSI, 1945 SE Water Avenue, is the wet-day science museum, submarine tour extra, streetcar stop at the door.

Tom McCall Waterfront Park is the 1.5-mile west-bank walk. Use it to get from the museum to the river. Do not schedule it as an attraction.

Getting there

TriMet lists a $2.80 adult tap fare. The Red Line is about 40 minutes from the airport to downtown, station near baggage claim. Check the last train if the flight is late.

Where to stay in Portland

Book the neighborhood for the evenings. The Pearl is a hotel choice. Hawthorne is a different trip.

Book the neighborhood for the evenings, not the postcard. In a January 2026 r/askportland thread, people choosing among NW 23rd, Buckman, and Mississippi for bars and food mostly picked Buckman, Kerns, or Mississippi, and described 23rd as the pretty street that gets sleepy after dark. A March 2026 thread said the Pearl feels more like converted warehouses than the residential streets, and that Hawthorne, Alberta, or Mississippi fit a “Portland” trip better. A June 2024 thread splits harder: some commenters say do not stay in Old Town or downtown at all, and name the Jupiter or an east-side rental; others say the Pearl hotels are the practical ones. Read that as a real argument, not a slogan.

If the trip is food and nights

Inner Southeast, between the river and about 39th, Hawthorne to Division. You can walk to more than one street of restaurants, and you are a bus or a bike from downtown rather than in the middle of it. Buckman and Kerns are the names that keep coming up for being close to evening plans without being the mall.

Jupiter Hotel, 800 E Burnside

This is the east-side motel people in that 2024 thread actually name. It sits on Burnside, next to the Doug Fir side of the block, which is why it suits a music weekend. The review split is the decision. Google is milder than Yelp. Tripadvisor’s summary of recent reviews praises the location and the retro rooms and then lists noise, thin amenities, and extra fees. An August 2026 review calls a room smoky and the parking add-on steep. A longer April 2026 review likes the art and still describes walking across the street to the newer NEXT building for water. Stay here if you want that block at night. Do not stay here if you want quiet and a simple breakfast.

Avg 3.6 Google 4.1 (1,616) · Yelp 3.1 (551) · Tripadvisor 3.5 (1,046) checked Oct 2026

KEX, 100 NE Martin Luther King Jr Blvd

A hostel that also has private rooms, with a restaurant and a sauna, a block from a bridge toward downtown. Tripadvisor’s small set of reviews (61) is enthusiastic about the lobby restaurant and the staff, and honest about hostel noise: one August 2026 review got moved off a room over the patio. Yelp’s 50 reviews sit much lower, so do not treat the Tripadvisor score as a large sample. It suits people who want a social lobby. It does not suit anyone who needs a generic quiet hotel room and no surprises at the desk. One September 2025 review disputes a smoking ban and a charge. That is one account, not a pattern, and it is the kind of complaint you should know exists.

Avg 4.2 Google 4.5 (368) · Yelp 3.5 (50) · Tripadvisor 4.7 (61) checked Oct 2026

If you want the quirky night near Alberta

McMenamins Kennedy School, 5736 NE 33rd

A former school with a hotel, bars, a movie theater, and a soaking pool, in Concordia rather than downtown. Google’s count is enormous. Yelp is cooler. The hotel listing on Tripadvisor, not the separate restaurant listing, is the one to read for a room. It suits a night you want to wander the building. It is a poor base if the days are the art museum and the waterfront, because you will ride in every morning. Askportland mentions it as the northeast stay when Alberta and Mississippi are the point.

Avg 4.3 Google 4.6 (8,977) · Yelp 3.9 (1,639) · Tripadvisor 4.3 (781) checked Oct 2026

If you still want a hotel in the center

Then pick the Pearl or the edge of downtown on purpose, and ignore the people who say the whole center is a write-off. You are choosing walkable Powell’s, the museum, and the airport train over a neighborhood street.

Canopy by Hilton, Pearl, 425 NW 9th

The practical Pearl hotel. Tripadvisor reviews from fall 2026 praise the staff and the location and then complain about valet-only parking, a tiny in-room coffee setup, and rooms that run hot. A Yelp search-result card showed a score, but not a listing URL clear enough to use, so Yelp is left off. Suits a first trip that wants the Pearl without a convention hotel. Trade-off: you are not on Hawthorne at dinner unless you ride.

Avg 4.4 Google 4.2 (908) · Tripadvisor 4.5 (588) checked Oct 2026

The Hoxton, 15 NW 4th, Old Town

Stylish rooms, a rooftop, and a staff that recent Tripadvisor reviews single out by name. The same reviews call the rooms small and the street noise real, and they say out loud that this is an urban block, not a quiet one. Yelp places it in Old Town–Chinatown. That is the trade. Askportland commenters who tell you to avoid Old Town are talking about this area. Stay here if you want a designed hotel and you are fine walking through that neighborhood at night. Stay in the Pearl or on Burnside if you are not.

Avg 4.3 Google 4.3 (1,144) · Yelp 4.3 (141) · Tripadvisor 4.4 (1,047) checked Oct 2026

What to skip as a default

Old Town as a base, unless the Hoxton’s design is the reason. Washington Park as a base. The airport, unless the Red Line’s last train does not work for your flight. Downtown is fine for two nights of museums. It is a dull place to spend four evenings, which is what those Reddit threads are actually saying.

Destinations

Destination guides will cover cities, towns, regions, coasts, and parks. This hub is the index of the launch map. Each cluster opens onto a page that says the guide is coming soon.

Coverage order follows the launch plan: eight clusters in each state. The order is a plan of work. It is a sequence for publishing, and the pages do not rank the places.

Big Sur and Carmel share a cluster. Napa and Sonoma share a cluster. Cannon Beach also sits inside the Oregon Coast cluster and has its own page. Washington islands is a cluster whose scope is still open, beside San Juan Islands and Whidbey Island.

Lake Tahoe is listed with California and covers the California side. The Columbia Gorge is listed with Oregon and also reaches Washington.

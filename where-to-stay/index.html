Where to stay

This section is for lodging and neighborhoods, written so a traveler can match a base to the trip. The comparisons are the point: who a stay suits, and what you give up.

A finished lodging guide is written to answer a few practical questions.

  • Who is this a fit for?
  • What do you give up by choosing it?
  • Does it suit a short weekend?
  • Is it practical for the way the traveler wants to get around?
  • How does the price sit next to the alternatives, once prices are checked?

No hotel, neighborhood ranking, or price is published in this version. Working titles below open the cluster page, where the guide is marked coming soon.

Hotel links are the planned way this section earns its keep. Any affiliate relationship will be labeled beside the link. There are no booking links here.
